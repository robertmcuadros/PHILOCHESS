This public inventory contains sixteen representative tests selected from the
complete sixty-four-test validation inventory for PHILOCHESS 0.2-alpha-011.
It preserves the approved identifiers, original numbering, FENs, move
sequences, expected results, and technical objectives of the selected tests.

The sample covers the six technical blocks of the complete inventory:
- historical-state loading and reconstruction;
- counter increments and resets;
- Position copying and branch isolation;
- thresholds, false positives, and terminal precedence;
- root, UCI, alpha-beta, and score propagation;
- quiescence and integration with other terminal rules.

Technical oracle used by the sample:
- The fifth FEN field is the halfmove clock.
- A pawn move or any capture resets the clock to 0.
- Every other legal move increments the clock by one.
- The sixth FEN field is independent of the fifty-move counter.
- FiftyMoveDraw is recognized when halfmoveClock >= 100.
- Checkmate, Stalemate, and DeadPosition retain their precedence and identity.
- A FiftyMoveDraw node returns score 0.
- Detection is integrated at the root, in alpha-beta, and in quiescence.
- At a legal root, UCI still receives a legal bestmove; 0000 remains reserved
  for roots without legal moves.

The identifiers use the form FMR-Xnn, where FMR means Fifty-Move Rule, X
identifies the technical family, and nn preserves the order inside its block.

FMR-H05 — Same initial placement with different history
Test 5 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Command under test:

    position startpos moves g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8

Counter sequence:

    g1f3  halfmoveClock: 0 -> 1
    g8f6  halfmoveClock: 1 -> 2
    f3g1  halfmoveClock: 2 -> 3
    f6g8  halfmoveClock: 3 -> 4

Equivalent FEN of the expected final state:

    rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 4 3

Expected final state:
- White to move.
- The white king is not in check.
- 20 legal moves.
- Castling rights: KQkq.
- En-passant target square: none.
- Halfmove clock: 4.
- Fullmove number: 3.

Reconstruction analysis:
- Both the white and black knights return to their initial squares.
- None of the four moves is a pawn move or capture.
- The placement, side to move, castling rights, and en-passant target square
  again match the initial position.
- The halfmove clock does not return to 0: it must retain the history of four
  reversible moves.
- The fullmove number increases after each black move and ends at 3.

Expected technical results:
- Complete sequence accepted: yes.
- Four moves applied legally: yes.
- Final placement identical to the initial position: yes.
- Final halfmove clock equal to 4: yes.
- Final fullmove number equal to 3: yes.
- Exact match with the equivalent final FEN: yes.

Technical objective:
Demonstrate that historical state cannot be inferred solely from placement
and that position startpos moves preserves the counter even when the pieces
return to their original squares.

Status:
VERIFIED

FMR-H06 — Reconstructed transition from 99 to 100
Test 6 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Command under test:

    position fen 7k/8/8/8/8/8/8/K1R5 w - - 99 50 moves c1c2

Counter sequence:

    Initial FEN state:  halfmoveClock =  99,   fullmoveNumber = 50
    c1c2:               halfmoveClock = 100,   fullmoveNumber = 50

Equivalent FEN of the expected final state:

    7k/8/8/8/8/8/2R5/K7 b - - 100 50

Expected final state:
- Black to move.
- The black king is not in check.
- 3 legal moves.
- Castling rights: none.
- En-passant target square: none.
- Halfmove clock: 100.
- Fullmove number: 50.

Reconstruction analysis:
- The position fen ... moves path must begin at 99, not at 0.
- c1c2 is a quiet rook move: it neither moves a pawn nor makes a capture.
- The move raises the counter exactly from 99 to 100.
- Because the move is made by White, the fullmove number remains at 50.
- The final position has legal moves; the test does not confuse the threshold
  with checkmate or stalemate.

Expected technical results:
- Base FEN accepted: yes.
- Move c1c2 accepted: yes.
- Final halfmove clock equal to 100: yes.
- Final fullmove number equal to 50: yes.
- Exact match with the equivalent final FEN: yes.
- Invocation of search as part of this test: no.

Technical objective:
Verify that position fen ... moves reconstructs exactly the operational
transition that completes 100 plies, without yet anticipating alpha-011
scoring or terminal precedence.

Status:
VERIFIED

FMR-B04 — Incrementing branch versus pawn-move branch
Test 28 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Parent FEN:

    7k/8/8/8/8/8/4P3/K1R5 w - - 99 50

Branch A — quiet move:

    c1c2

Expected FEN for A:

    7k/8/8/8/8/8/2R1P3/K7 b - - 100 50

Branch B — pawn move:

    e2e4

Expected FEN for B:

    7k/8/8/8/4P3/8/8/K1R5 b - e3 0 50

Isolation checks:
- A must reach 100 from parent value 99.
- B must reset to 0 from the same parent value 99.
- The e3 en-passant target square exists only in B.
- The parent retains the pawn on e2, rook on c1, and halfmove clock 99.
- A–B and B–A order must not alter the oracles.

Technical objective:
Demonstrate that a branch reaching the threshold and another resetting the
counter can coexist without transmitting the halfmove clock or en-passant
state to each other.

Status:
VERIFIED

FMR-B08 — Recursive isolation at two plies
Test 32 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Root FEN:

    7k/8/8/8/8/8/4P3/K1R5 w - - 98 50

Branch A:

    c1c2 h8h7

Expected FEN after c1c2:

    7k/8/8/8/8/8/2R1P3/K7 b - - 99 50

Expected FEN after c1c2 h8h7:

    8/7k/8/8/8/8/2R1P3/K7 w - - 100 51

Branch B:

    e2e4 h8h7

Expected FEN after e2e4:

    7k/8/8/8/4P3/8/8/K1R5 b - e3 0 50

Expected FEN after e2e4 h8h7:

    8/7k/8/8/4P3/8/8/K1R5 w - - 1 51

Isolation checks:
- Child A1 must retain 99 after grandchild A2 with value 100 is created.
- Child B1 must retain 0 and the e3 target square after B2 with value 1 and
  no en-passant target square is created.
- The root must retain halfmove clock 98 and its complete FEN.
- Complete exploration of A cannot change B1 or B2, and vice versa.
- A–B and B–A order must produce the same four descendants.

Technical objective:
Extend the isolation check beyond the root and demonstrate that each recursive
level preserves its own state while building descendants.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    position fen 7k/8/8/8/8/8/4P3/K1R5 w - - 99 73
    depth 2

Search structure:
- White has a rook and a pawn against a lone king.
- The root has 18 legal moves.
- 16 king or rook moves produce FiftyMoveDraw children with score 0.
- e2e3 and e2e4 reset the counter and retain the material advantage.
- The pawn moves appear after the drawing branches in the stable order before
  move ordering.

Expected results:
- The first drawing branch does not stop the root loop.
- Final root score: positive.
- bestmove: e2e4.
- Reference score with the current evaluation: +614 cp.
- Selection of a quiet move that reaches 100: no.

Technical objective:
Demonstrate that PHILOCHESS avoids completing the rule when it has a
