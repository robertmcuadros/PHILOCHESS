1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
==============================================================================

This document presents a representative public sample of 16 positions taken
from the complete private inventory of 64 verified tests prepared for:

    PHILOCHESS 0.2-alpha-009 — Stalemate Recognition in Search.

The sample makes it possible to document publicly the principles, diversity,
and rigor of the inventory without publishing the complete private suite. It
does not replace the 64-test inventory and is not an independent reduced test
suite.

The original identifiers and numbering of the selected positions are
preserved, together with their FENs, analyses, rules-based results, technical
oracles, objectives, and VERIFIED statuses.
