2. TECHNICAL BASIS AND ORACLES
==============================================================================

2.1 Basis in the Laws of Chess and position identity

The threefold repetition rule concerns positions, not the literal repetition
of moves. The appearances need not be consecutive or separated by the same
move sequence.

For the purposes of this inventory, two positions are considered identical
only when all of the following match:

- pieces of the same type and color occupy the same squares;
- the same player has the move;
- the same castling rights exist;
- the same legal en-passant capture is effectively available.

Rules reference:
https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023

Visual equality of the board is insufficient. A change of turn, loss of a
castling right, or the existence of a legal en-passant capture can make two
positions with the same piece placement different.

2.2 Conceptual repetition key

The identity represented by PHILOCHESS can be expressed conceptually as:

    piece placement | side to move | castling rights | effective en passant

The implementation may use a position key or any equivalent representation,
provided that it preserves this identity exactly.

The halfmove clock and fullmove number are not part of a position's identity
for threefold repetition. Their values may change between appearances without
preventing the positions from being identical under the rules.

2.3 Effective en-passant capture

The mere textual presence of an en-passant target square in the FEN must not
automatically create a different position. That information distinguishes
identity only when the player to move actually has a legal en-passant capture.

This block covers both the case in which the en-passant capture exists and the
case in which the square is recorded but no capture is possible.

2.4 History and scope of an isolated FEN

PHILOCHESS can receive a position through:

    position startpos moves <sequence>
    position fen <complete FEN> moves <sequence>

When a move sequence is supplied, the positions must be reconstructed in order
and retained as part of the history relevant to repetition.

An isolated FEN does not encode how many times the position appeared earlier.
It therefore cannot by itself prove a second or third historical appearance.
This limitation is tested explicitly in TRR-H04.

2.5 Occurrence counting and threshold

P0 denotes the initial position of the sequence, before the first move. The
position after the first move is ply 1, and so on.

The current position is part of the history and must be counted exactly once.
For the alpha-012 oracle:

    occurrenceCount = 1  -> no threefold repetition
    occurrenceCount = 2  -> no threefold repetition
    occurrenceCount = 3  -> threefoldDetected = true

Earlier appearances may be separated by different positions and different
move sequences.

2.6 Common oracle for the block

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- complete legal reconstruction of the UCI sequence;
- exact final FEN;
- identification of the relevant appearances by ply number;
- equality or inequality of the expected conceptual key;
- exact occurrenceCount for the root position;
- exact value of threefoldDetected;
- absence of false positives caused by turn, castling, or en passant;
- absence of false negatives caused by the halfmove clock, fullmove number, or
  a merely nominal en-passant target square.

No test in the first block requires a score, PV, bestmove, static evaluation,
alpha-beta search, or quiescence search.

2.7 Identifier convention

Identifiers in the first block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Inn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- I means Identity or position identity;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

2.8 Reconstruction of history communicated through UCI

Each position command defines from scratch the base position and move sequence
that PHILOCHESS must reconstruct. The relevant history contains P0 and one
additional position after each legal move in the sequence. Therefore:

    historyPositionCount = number of moves + 1

The startpos form uses the standard initial position as P0. The fen form uses
exactly the supplied FEN as P0; the engine has no information about positions
preceding that FEN.

2.9 History replacement and separation between games

A new position command completely replaces the history built by the preceding
position command. This replacement must occur even when the interface has not
sent ucinewgame.

When ucinewgame is received, any historical or temporary state from the
previous game must remain separate from the new game. A later isready command
only synchronizes communication and adds no positions to history.

2.10 Immutability of UCI history during go

The go command starts a search on the current position, but it neither executes
the bestmove nor modifies the history supplied by the position command.
Positions generated during search belong to the temporary variation path and
must be removed when moves are unmade.

If several go commands are sent without a new position command, the root
remains the same and its history entry must not be duplicated. This property
prevents contamination between searches and keeps the root, which is already
the last UCI-history entry, from being counted twice.

2.11 Boundary after irreversible moves

A pawn move or capture prevents the current position from being identical to a
position preceding that irreversible change. Repetition counting may be
limited to the segment after the last irreversible move, provided that the
position reached immediately after that move is included as the first entry of
the new segment.

This inventory requires the correct functional result; it does not impose a
specific internal structure for storing or traversing history.

2.12 Common oracle for Block 2

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- legal and ordered reconstruction of P0 and all communicated plies;
- exact historyPositionCount;
- exact final FEN;
- exact occurrenceCount for the current position;
- exact value of threefoldDetected;
- complete replacement of history after a new position command;
- absence of historical information invented from an isolated FEN;
- absence of changes produced by ucinewgame, isready, or go beyond each
  command's specific contract;
- absence of root duplication and residual state from earlier searches or
  games;
- correct counting within the segment after the last irreversible move.

No test in the second block yet requires a score, PV, or specific bestmove.
Integration of the draw result at the root is tested in Block 3; detection
inside the tree is tested in later blocks.

2.13 Block 2 identifier convention

Identifiers in the second block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Hnn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- H means History or UCI history;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

2.14 Threefold repetition already present at the root

When the key of the current position appears three times in the relevant UCI
history, PHILOCHESS must recognize that a draw may be claimed under the rules
before starting ordinary search. For alpha-012, the oracle is:

    rootThreefoldDetected = true
    rootScore = 0
    UCI score = cp 0

The third appearance permits a draw claim but does not mean that the root has
no legal moves. PHILOCHESS must preserve its UCI contract and respond to go
with a legal bestmove.

2.15 Draw shortcut and work permitted at the root

Once threefold repetition is recognized at the root, PHILOCHESS may generate
the legal moves needed to select a UCI response, but it must not perform static
evaluation or enter alpha-beta or quiescence search.

The instrumented oracles are:

    staticEvalCalls = 0
    alphaBetaCalls  = 0
    quiescenceCalls = 0

No specific nodes, time, or depth value is required in the information line,
because those fields may depend on the reporting system. Nor is a PV required
when ordinary search is not executed.

2.16 Legal bestmove and use of 0000

At a repeated root with legal moves:

    bestmove belongs to the set of legal moves
    bestmove != 0000

The inventory does not prescribe a specific move when several equivalent legal
responses exist. It may prescribe one when the root has only one legal move.

bestmove 0000 is reserved for a root with no legal moves, such as checkmate or
stalemate. The control in this block uses stalemate to verify the UCI boundary
without turning it into an exhaustive precedence test among rules; that
regression is completed in Block 6.

2.17 go modes and information reporting

Root detection must be independent of the mode that starts the search. This
block covers:

    go depth <n>
    go movetime <ms>
    go wtime <ms> btime <ms> winc <ms> binc <ms>

Specific notices issued through info string are outside alpha-012 and are
reserved for alpha-013 integration work. This block requires only the neutral
score and the corresponding bestmove.

2.18 Common oracle for Block 3

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- exact occurrenceCount and threefoldDetected at the root;
- rootScore = 0 and score cp 0 when threefold repetition exists;
- absence of static evaluation, alpha-beta, and quiescence after recognition
  of threefold repetition at the root;
- legal bestmove other than 0000 when legal moves exist;
- exact bestmove when only one legal move exists;
- ordinary search active when only two appearances exist;
- equivalent behavior with White and Black to move;
- stability with go depth, go movetime, and UCI time control;
- preservation of history and result across successive searches;
- bestmove 0000 only in the control with no legal moves;
- preservation of the stalemate diagnosis in the terminal control.

No draw-claim info string, specific PV, or specific bestmove is required when
the root has more than one legal move.

2.19 Block 3 identifier convention

Identifiers in the third block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Rnn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- R means Root or search root;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

2.20 Combined history during search

At an internal node, the relevant count combines two sources:

1. the permanent history reconstructed through position, whose last entry is
   the current root;
2. temporary positions generated after the root in the active line.

The root must not be added a second time when alpha-beta starts. For a target
node, the conceptual oracle is:

    combinedOccurrenceCount =
        appearances in UCI history
      + appearances after the root in the active line

The target node position is included exactly once as the line's last entry.
Positions in already abandoned branches are not part of the count.

2.21 Detection in alpha-beta

When an alpha-beta node reaches combinedOccurrenceCount = 3, PHILOCHESS must
recognize the draw before static evaluation, ordinary child expansion, and any
later entry into quiescence. The local oracle is:

    alphaBetaThreefoldDetected = true
    nodeScore = 0
    staticEvalCallsAtTarget = 0
    childExpansionCallsAtTarget = 0
    quiescenceCallsFromTarget = 0

The block tests the result at the repeated node. Complete propagation of that
value to the root and bestmove selection are reserved for Block 5.

2.22 Detection upon entering quiescence search

Quiescence search must check repetition upon node entry, before calculating
stand pat or generating tactical continuations. When the entry node is the
third appearance:

    quiescenceThreefoldDetected = true
    quiescenceScore = 0
    standPatEvalCallsAtTarget = 0
    quiescenceChildCallsAtTarget = 0

Sequences leading to these nodes are generated by alpha-beta search.
Quiescence is not required to produce silent-move cycles by itself.

2.23 Branch isolation and temporary-stack restoration

Each move added to the line must add exactly one temporary position, and each
unmake must remove exactly the corresponding entry. This remains mandatory
when a return occurs because of threefold repetition or causes an alpha-beta
cutoff.

After leaving a branch:

    temporaryPathDepth = value before the branch
    historyPositionCount = value before the search

A position visited in one sibling branch cannot contribute to the count of the
next branch. This contract also applies to successive quiescence calls in
different branches.

2.24 Common oracle for Block 4

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- exact FEN of the root and target node;
- historyPositionCount before search begins;
- appearances from UCI history and the active line;
- exact combinedOccurrenceCount at the target node;
- positive detection only on the third appearance;
- local score 0 in alpha-beta or quiescence when threefold repetition exists;
- absence of static evaluation, stand pat, and child expansion after detection;
- normal continuation of search when only two appearances exist;
- absence of root double counting;
- isolation between sibling branches;
- complete restoration of the temporary stack after an ordinary return, a
  repetition return, or an alpha-beta cutoff;
- immutability of permanent UCI history.

The internal harness is instrumented to ensure that target lines are visited
at the indicated depth and search phase. The bestmove, PV, and final root score
are not oracles for this block.

2.25 Block 4 identifier convention

Identifiers in the fourth block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Snn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- S means Search or detection inside search;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

TRR-S01 through TRR-S08 apply to alpha-beta. TRR-S09 through TRR-S16 apply to
quiescence search.

2.26 Controlled tree and Block 5 score convention

The strategic tests use an instrumented tree with legal moves and an explicit
set of candidates at the root. The repetitive branch is reconstructed through
a legal sequence and obtains score 0 exclusively upon reaching the third
appearance.

Nonrepetitive alternatives may receive controlled values to isolate comparison
and propagation behavior from future changes in PST, mobility, or other
evaluation terms. When a test uses an actual checkmate or a dead-position
termination, candidateScore is derived from that rules-based result rather
than from an injected value.

candidateScore always denotes the value already propagated to the perspective
of the side to move at the root. Therefore:

    candidateScore > 0  -> favorable result for the root side
    candidateScore = 0  -> neutral result
    candidateScore < 0  -> unfavorable result for the root side

At a root with Black to move, a positive value favors Black. The harness may
limit examined candidates to those specified by each test, but all of them
must be legal moves in the root FEN.

2.27 Propagation of zero in negamax

The node that recognizes the third appearance returns exactly 0. In negamax,
each level reverses the sign of the received value, but:

    -0 = 0

Consequently, the neutral score must pass unchanged through any number of
levels and remain unchanged when moving from quiescence to alpha-beta and from
alpha-beta to the root. It must not become +1, -1, an incomplete bound, or a
static evaluation of the repeated position.

2.28 Strategic ordering of alternatives

The expected chess behavior arises from ordinary score comparison; it requires
no separate rules based on labels such as winning or losing. The conceptual
order is:

    forced mate > positive score > draw = 0
                  > negative score > forced mating loss

PHILOCHESS must therefore avoid a repetitive branch when an alternative above
0 exists, choose it when all other alternatives are below 0, and be able to
accept it when no alternative is better.

2.29 Ties between equivalent alternatives

When a repetitive branch and a nonrepetitive branch both receive
candidateScore = 0, the oracle accepts both as valid best moves. The inventory
does not require an artificial tiebreak or a specific bestmove. It does
require:

    rootScore = 0
    bestmove belongs to the set of candidates with score 0

A stable ordering policy may retain the first move or select another equivalent
one without constituting a failure.

In TRR-D13 and TRR-D14, the nonrepetitive alternative reaches the dead position
K+N against K. Its candidateScore = 0 is therefore an exact result under the
rules, not an approximation of static evaluation.

2.30 Common oracle for Block 5

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- legality of the FEN, history, and all root candidates;
- occurrenceCount = 2 at the root before searching;
- combinedOccurrenceCount = 3 in the repetitive branch;
- local score 0 at the repeated node;
- exact negation and propagation of zero to candidateScore = 0;
- exact controlled value of every instrumented leaf or exact terminal value of
  each mate or dead-position branch;
- rootScore equal to the maximum candidateScore from the perspective of the
  side to move;
- selection of the positive alternative over the draw;
- selection of the draw over negative alternatives;
- selection of a forced mate over the draw;
- selection of the draw over a forced mating loss;
- symmetry with White and Black to move;
- independence from the order in which candidates are examined;
- no requirement for a unique bestmove when several alternatives tie for the
  maximum value.

These tests do observe propagation to the root and the strategic choice among
different candidates. They do not replace precedence rules applied within a
single terminal node or the complete cross-rule regression reserved for
alpha-013.

2.31 Block 5 identifier convention

Identifiers in the fifth block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Dnn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- D means Decision or strategic decision;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

TRR-D01 through TRR-D04 test propagation. TRR-D05 through TRR-D08 test rejection
of the draw when a superior continuation exists. TRR-D09 through TRR-D12 test
seeking the draw against inferior alternatives. TRR-D13 through TRR-D16 test
acceptance and the boundaries around score 0.

2.32 Block 6 identity edge cases

Block 6 extends identity controls without repeating the basic cases from the
first block. TRR-X01 requires full legality checking of an apparently available
en-passant capture: the presence of an en-passant target square and an adjacent
pawn is insufficient if the capture exposes the moving side's king. TRR-X02
requires castling rights to remain part of the key even when castling is
temporarily prevented and only one of those rights is lost.

2.33 Behavior beyond the threshold and continuity of the UCI response

TRR-X03 uses four appearances to verify that the functional condition is
occurrenceCount >= 3 and does not deactivate after the third. If the interface
does not adjudicate the draw, PHILOCHESS retains score cp 0 and returns a legal
bestmove other than 0000. Automatic fivefold repetition is not part of this
inventory.

2.34 Special irreversible transitions

TRR-X04, TRR-X05, and TRR-X06 test, respectively, an ordinary capture made by a
piece, an executed en-passant capture, and a noncapturing promotion. In all
three cases, the position reached immediately after the last irreversible move
must be retained as the first valid appearance of the new segment. The harness
must verify the exact FEN, halfmove-clock reset, complete board update, and
count of later returns.

2.35 Immediate detection and regression based on the game against Zoidberg

TRR-X07 places the third appearance at search ply 1 and requires it to be
recognized in alpha-beta with positive remaining depth. TRR-X08 reconstructs
96 plies from the PHILOCHESS 0.2-alpha-008–Zoidberg 0.1 game and fixes the
root after 48...Kd7.
The continuation 49.Rb7+ Kd6 reaches the third appearance and receives
candidateScore = 0; the controlled alternative 49.Rb8 preserves a score of
+480 and must be selected.

2.36 Common oracle for Block 6

Each test must verify, as applicable:

- legality of the initial FEN and every communicated sequence;
- exact historyPositionCount, occurrenceCount, and combinedOccurrenceCount;
- normalization of en passant according to its effective legality;
- partial and permanent update of castling rights;
- preservation of detection when occurrenceCount reaches 4;
- inclusion of the position immediately after an ordinary capture, an
  en-passant capture, or a promotion as the first entry of the new segment;
- detection at the first node after the root before evaluation or expansion;
- exact reconstruction of the critical plies in the reference game;
- local score 0 for the repetitive branch and selection of the positive
  controlled alternative in the regression against Zoidberg;
- absence of interference from the fifty-move rule, automatic fivefold
  repetition, and UCI notices reserved for later work.

2.37 Block 6 identifier convention

Identifiers in the sixth block follow the pattern:

    TRR-Xnn

where:

- TRR means Threefold Repetition Rule;
- X identifies false positives, edge cases, and specific regressions;
- nn represents the two-digit sequential number.

TRR-X01 through TRR-X03 cover identity and behavior beyond the threshold.
TRR-X04 through TRR-X06 cover special irreversible transitions. TRR-X07 tests
the first-node boundary within search, and TRR-X08 reproduces the historical

TRR-I01 — Exact third appearance of the initial position
Test 1 of 16
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
  Basic positive control.

Technical objective:
  Confirm that the initial position is recognized as having appeared for the
  third time after two legal knight cycles.

UCI command:
  position startpos moves g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8 g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8

Resulting root FEN:
  rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 8 5

Relevant appearances:
  1. Ply 0:
     rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1
  2. Ply 4:
     rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 4 3
  3. Ply 8:
     rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 8 5

Expected result:
  occurrenceCount = 3
  threefoldDetected = true

PASS criterion:
  The three positions produce the same repetition key, and the current
  position is recognized as the third appearance.

FAIL criterion:
  The counter returns a value other than 3 or detection is not activated.

Note:
  The FEN counters change among the three appearances but do not alter the
  identity of the position under the rules.

TRR-I05 — Same placement but different castling rights
Test 2 of 16
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
  Negative control for historical identity.

Technical objective:
  Confirm that an earlier position with castling rights is not counted as
  identical to another in which those rights have been lost, even though the
  kings and rooks have returned to their original squares.

UCI command:
  position fen r3k2r/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K2R w KQkq - 0 1 moves e1f1 e8f8 f1e1 f8e8 e1f1 e8f8 f1e1 f8e8

Resulting root FEN:
  r3k2r/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K2R w - - 8 5

States with the same placement and White to move:
  Ply 0, with castling rights:
    r3k2r/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K2R w KQkq - 0 1
  Ply 4, without castling rights:
    r3k2r/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K2R w - - 4 3
  Ply 8, without castling rights:
    r3k2r/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K2R w - - 8 5

Expected result for the root key:
  occurrenceCount = 2
  threefoldDetected = false

PASS criterion:
  The ply-0 position is excluded because it retains KQkq; only plies 4 and 8
  are counted.

FAIL criterion:
  The engine compares only piece placement and side to move, counts ply 0 as
  well, and declares a nonexistent threefold repetition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    7k/8/8/3pP3/8/8/N7/7K w - - 4 4

    7k/8/8/3pP3/8/8/N7/7K w - - 8 6

Expected result for the root key:
  occurrenceCount = 2
  threefoldDetected = false

PASS criterion:
  Ply 1 receives a different key because e5xd6 en passant is a legal move;

TRR-H01 — Complete reconstruction through position startpos moves
Test 4 of 16
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
  Positive reconstruction control from the initial position.

Technical objective:
  Confirm that PHILOCHESS reconstructs and retains in order every position
  communicated through position startpos moves, including the position reached
  after pawn moves e2e3 and e7e6.

  position startpos moves e2e3 e7e6 g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8 g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8

  rnbqkbnr/pppp1ppp/4p3/8/8/4P3/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 8 6

Reconstructed history:
  historyPositionCount = 11

Relevant appearances of the root position:
  1. Ply 2:
     rnbqkbnr/pppp1ppp/4p3/8/8/4P3/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 2
  2. Ply 6:
     rnbqkbnr/pppp1ppp/4p3/8/8/4P3/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 4 4
  3. Ply 10:
     rnbqkbnr/pppp1ppp/4p3/8/8/4P3/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 8 6

Expected result:
  occurrenceCount = 3
  threefoldDetected = true

PASS criterion:
  P0 and all ten plies are reconstructed in the received order; the current
