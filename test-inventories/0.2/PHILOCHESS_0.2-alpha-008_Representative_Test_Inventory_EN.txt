==============================================================================
1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
==============================================================================

Mobility v1 is the first explicit evaluation of piece mobility in PHILOCHESS
and the third independent static-evaluation layer:

    Static evaluation = Material + PST v1 + Mobility v1

The selected controlled positions isolate geometry, board occupancy, blockers,
enemy captures, the enemy-king exception, absolute pins, and attacked
destinations. The transversal positions verify bilateral cancellation and the
composition of material, PHILOCHESS PST v1, and Mobility v1. The integrated
positions verify all eligible pieces in the initial position and the occupancy
changes produced by 1.e4.

This representative subset does not replace the complete internal 64-position
inventory or reduce the scope of the validation performed for alpha-008.

==============================================================================
2. APPROVED MOBILITY V1 RULES
==============================================================================

2.1 Evaluated pieces

Mobility v1 evaluates:
- knights;
- bishops;
- rooks;
- queens.

Mobility v1 excludes:
- pawns;
- kings.

2.2 Counting model

Mobility v1 counts occupancy-based pseudo-legal destinations:

- Every reachable empty square counts.
- A square occupied by a friendly piece does not count.
- A friendly piece stops a sliding piece's ray.
- The first square occupied by an enemy knight, bishop, rook, queen, or pawn
  counts as a capture destination.
- The first square occupied by the enemy king does not count.
- An enemy piece stops a sliding piece's ray.
- No square beyond any blocker counts.
- A knight jumps directly to its destination; therefore, it has no intervening
  ray that can be stopped.
- Squares attacked by the opponent still count.
- Absolute pins do not reduce the count.
- Destination safety is not examined.
- The result is independent of the side to move.

Mobility v1 deliberately measures piece geometry and board occupancy. It does
not assess full move legality, king exposure, destination quality, pawn
structure, or king safety.

2.3 Centered linear scale

For each evaluated piece:

    Piece adjustment = weight x (destinations - reference)

Approved parameters:

    Piece    Geometric maximum    Reference    Weight
    Knight           8                4         4 cp
    Bishop          13                7         3 cp
    Rook            14                7         2 cp
    Queen           27               14         1 cp

The bilateral Mobility v1 score is:

    Mobility v1 =
        Sum of White piece adjustments
        minus
        Sum of Black piece adjustments

All expected scores in this inventory are expressed from White's perspective.
