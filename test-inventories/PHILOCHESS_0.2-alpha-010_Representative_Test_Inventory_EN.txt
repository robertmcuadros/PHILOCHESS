1. PURPOSE AND SELECTION CRITERIA
==============================================================================

This document is a public sample of the internal inventory used to validate:

    PHILOCHESS 0.2-alpha-010 — Dead-Position Recognition in Search.

The complete suite contains 64 tests. This sample retains 16 of them: four
from each block of the original inventory and eight for each side to move.
The identifiers, FENs, and oracles come from the complete inventory. Gaps in
the numbering are deliberate: conceptual variety takes priority over
identifier continuity.

    Block 1 — Dead positions by material                         4 tests
    Block 2 — Structural blockade or immobility                  4 tests
    Block 3 — Boundaries and false positives                     4 tests
    Block 4 — Transitions and search integration                 4 tests
                                                                --------
    Total                                                       16 tests

Selected tests:

    DPR-M01  DPR-M04  DPR-M05  DPR-M12
    DPR-S01  DPR-S06  DPR-S13  DPR-S16
    DPR-N01  DPR-N12  DPR-N17  DPR-N20
    DPR-T01  DPR-T04  DPR-T11  DPR-T14

The sample does not replace the complete inventory or reproduce every
color-reversed pair. Derived nodes and transitions documented within a test
are internal oracles of that test; they are not counted as additional tests.

==============================================================================
2. COMMON ORACLES
==============================================================================

Blocks 1 and 2 — Dead positions:

- neither player can checkmate the opponent's king by any possible series of
  legal moves;
- the regulatory result is a draw by dead position;
- the expected score at the root, in alpha-beta, and in quiescence is 0;
- static evaluation and stand pat must not run after terminal recognition;
- if the root retains legal moves and the GUI requests one, PHILOCHESS must
  return a legal bestmove other than 0000.

Block 3 — Negative controls:

- at least one legal series ends in checkmate;
- the position is not dead, and normal search must continue;
- the dead-position rule must not impose a score of 0.

Block 4 — Integration:

- the verdict must be calculated from the updated state of every node;
- dead positions created within alpha-beta or quiescence must return 0 before
  static evaluation or stand pat;
- checkmate retains its precedence, terminal cause, and score.

The legal-move counts in this sample were made before applying dead-position
termination.

3. BLOCK 1 — DEAD POSITIONS BY MATERIAL
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-M01 — White king versus Black king
Original position: 1 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/8/8/8/8/2k5/8/K7 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 2 (Ka2 and Kb1).

Oracle:
Both sides retain only the king. No pawn can promote, and no legal series can
allow either player to construct checkmate. The existence of two legal moves
rules out stalemate.

Expected result:
Dead position true; stalemate false; checkmate false; score 0 at the root, in
alpha-beta, and in quiescence; legal bestmove other than 0000 if requested.

Technical objective:
Verify the most elementary material recognition with legal moves available.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-M04 — Black king in check against White king and bishop
Original position: 4 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/8/8/4B3/8/8/8/K7 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: yes. Legal moves: 2 (Kh7 and Kg8).

Oracle:
Black retains only the king. White retains a king and one dark-squared bishop,
material incapable of checkmating a bare king under any legal cooperation.
The current check is not checkmate because two evasions exist.

Expected result:
Dead position true; stalemate false; checkmate false; score 0 at the root, in
alpha-beta, and in quiescence. Search must not examine the evasions deeply
after the verdict; the UCI layer must only select a legal evasion.

Technical objective:
Verify that a dead position may contain a check with evasions and that its
treatment does not depend on the color of the side to move.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-M05 — White king and knight versus Black king
Original position: 5 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/8/8/8/8/2N5/8/K7 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 11.

Oracle:
A king and lone knight cannot checkmate a bare king by any legal series. The
Black king alone cannot deliver checkmate either. The knight's full mobility
confirms that dead position does not mean immobility.

Expected result:
Dead position true; stalemate false; checkmate false; score 0 at the root, in
alpha-beta, and in quiescence. The knight's material value and mobility must
not reach static evaluation or stand pat.

Technical objective:
Verify the K+N versus K class with the minor piece fully mobile.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-M12 — Two light-squared bishops per side
Original position: 12 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/5b2/b7/8/6B1/8/1K6/3B4 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: no. Legal moves: 19.

Oracle:
All four bishops remain on the light-square complex. No sequence can create
other material or transfer a bishop to the dark-square complex; therefore,
neither side can construct checkmate.

Expected result:
Dead position true; stalemate false; checkmate false; score 0 at the root, in
alpha-beta, and in quiescence. Material, PST, and mobility must not replace
the terminal value of 0.

Technical objective:
Verify the broad material family with several legally promoted bishops, all
restricted to the same square complex.

Status: VERIFIED

4. BLOCK 2 — STRUCTURAL BLOCKADE OR IMMOBILITY
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-S01 — Dispersed barrier of three pawn pairs
Original position: 13 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/2k5/8/1p1p2p1/1P1P2P1/8/2K5/8 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 8.

Structural invariant:
The b4-b5, d4-d5, and g4-g5 pairs are blocked. Their cross-attacks cover the
unoccupied squares in the dividing zone. No pawn can advance or capture, and
the kings remain in disjoint regions.

Expected result:
Dead position true; pawns with future mobility: none; score 0 at the root, in
alpha-beta, and in quiescence; legal bestmove other than 0000 if requested.

Technical objective:
Verify that a dispersed barrier can be absolute through square control even
when it does not occupy every file.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-S06 — Pawn barrier with a confined Black bishop
Original position: 18 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/5k2/3b4/p1p1p1p1/P1P1P1P1/8/5K2/8 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: no. Legal moves: 12.

Structural invariant:
The alternating barrier permanently separates the kings. The Black bishop on
d6 can maneuver only in the upper sector; its downward routes end at the
Black pawns on c5 and e5. It cannot reach the White king or release a pawn.

Expected result:
Dead position true; bishop permanently confined; no pawn with future
mobility; score 0 before material, PST, mobility, or stand pat.

Technical objective:
Verify a mobile piece that is structurally unable to interact with the
opposing king.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-S13 — Compact kingside blockade; single legal move
Original position: 25 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/7p/k4p1P/5p1K/5Pp1/6P1/6P1/8 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 1 (Kh4).

Structural invariant:
The f4-f5, g3-g4, and h6-h7 pairs are blocked; g2 is stopped by its own pawn.
After Kh4, no advance, capture, or promotion is possible, and the structural
separation of the kings remains.

Expected result:
Dead position true; stalemate false; score 0. The only legal move must not
undergo deep search; if the GUI requests it, Kh4 must be returned.

Technical objective:
Verify terminal recognition before following a single continuation that does
not change bilateral checkmate impossibility.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-S16 — Complete stepped barrier; Black king in check
Original position: 28 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/7p/p5pP/Pp2kpP1/1Pp1pP2/2PpP3/3P4/2K5 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: yes. Legal moves: 3 (Kd6, Ke6, and Kd5).

Structural invariant:
All eight pawn pairs are blocked head-on, and no capture can change the
structure. All three evasions keep the Black king in its region; no promotion,
en passant capture, or interaction capable of constructing checkmate can
occur.

Expected result:
Dead position true; checkmate false; stalemate false; score 0. The in-check
path must not search the evasions deeply after the verdict.

Technical objective:
Verify a check with evasions inside a structurally dead position.

Status: VERIFIED

5. BLOCK 3 — BOUNDARIES AND FALSE POSITIVES
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-N01 — White king and two knights versus Black king
Original position: 29 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/8/5NK1/4N3/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 20.

Checkmate witness series:
1. Nf7#

Oracle:
Two knights cannot force checkmate against correct defense, but a legal
checkmate configuration exists with cooperation. The position is therefore
not dead.

Expected result:
Detector false at the root, in alpha-beta, and in quiescence; normal
evaluation and search must continue; bestmove other than 0000.

Technical objective:
Avoid confusing inability to force checkmate with the absolute impossibility
of reaching it.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-N12 — Unilateral impossibility: Black rook versus White king
Original position: 40 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/8/8/8/8/1k6/2r5/K7 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: no. Legal moves: 19.

Checkmate witness series:
1... Rc1#

Oracle:
White retains only the king and cannot deliver checkmate, but Black can do so
immediately. Impossibility must be established for both players; unilateral
impossibility is insufficient.

Expected result:
Dead position false; the rule must not impose 0; normal evaluation and search
must continue.

Technical objective:
Verify the bilateral conjunction when only one side lacks every checkmating
possibility.

Status: VERIFIED

DPR-N17 — Apparent blockade broken by a White en passant capture
Original position: 45 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
4k3/8/1p1p4/1PpP1p1p/2P2P1P/8/8/4K3 w - c6 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 7.

Checkmate witness series:
1. bxc6 e.p. Kf8 2. c7 Ke7 3. c5 dxc5
4. c8=Q Kd6 5. Qd8#

Oracle:
The c6 target square preserves the en passant right. Both bxc6 e.p. and
dxc6 e.p. break the only barrier, enable promotion, and preserve a legal
checkmate series. If that right expires, the resulting structure is dead.

Expected result:
Dead position false; the en passant target square must form part of the
analyzed state; normal search and bestmove other than 0000.

Technical objective:
Prevent an apparent barrier from being accepted while a legal en passant
capture can open it.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-N20 — Buchanan boundary; checkmate possible for Black
Original position: 48 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
8/8/7p/1p6/6pP/1p1p1pP1/1P1P1PkB/2B1K1Bb b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: no. Legal moves: 3 (Kh3, h5, and b4).

Checkmate witness series:
1... Kh3 2. Kd1 Bg2 3. Ke1 Bf1 4. Kd1 Be2+ 5. Ke1 Bd1
6. Kf1 Bc2 7. Ke1 Bb1 8. Kd1 Kg2 9. Ke1 b4 10. Kd1 Kf1
11. h5 Bc2#

Oracle:
The Black king's penetration, the b-pawn advance, and cooperation by the
White h-pawn allow the Black bishop to reach c2 and deliver checkmate. The
structure is a live boundary, not a dead position.

Expected result:
Dead position false; the rule must not impose 0; normal evaluation and search;
bestmove other than 0000.

Technical objective:
Protect the Buchanan structural boundary and the potential mobility of kings,
pawns, and bishops.

Status: VERIFIED

6. BLOCK 4 — TRANSITIONS AND SEARCH INTEGRATION

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-T01 — Dead position at the root with a White bishop
Original position: 49 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/8/8/8/8/3B4/8/1K6 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 15.

Integration oracle:
The position is already dead at the root. Recognition must precede material,
PST, mobility, stand pat, and deep search. If the GUI requests a move, only
one of the legal continuations must be selected.

Expected result:
Dead position true; score 0; evaluation and deep search not run; legal
bestmove other than 0000 if requested.

Technical objective:
Check detector placement at the root and replacement of a nonzero static
evaluation with the regulatory terminal value of 0.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-T04 — Black capture removes the last piece capable of checkmate
Original position: 52 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Root FEN:
8/8/8/3k4/3R4/8/8/7K b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: yes. Legal moves: 5.

Transition under test:
1... Kxd4

Child-node FEN:
8/8/8/8/3k4/8/8/7K w - - 0 2

Integration oracle:
The root is live because the White rook retains checkmating capability. Kxd4
is a legal evasion that leaves king versus king; the child node must be
recognized as dead before evaluating or searching the White king's three
moves.

Expected result:
Live root; dead child node; alpha-beta score 0; subsequent evaluation not run;
unmaking the move exactly restores the position, check, and turn.

Technical objective:
Verify recognition from the updated state of an alpha-beta child node.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-T11 — Dead position reached by a chain in quiescence
Original position: 59 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Root FEN:
8/4k3/4r3/4R3/8/8/8/K7 w - - 0 1

Turn: White. Check: no. Legal moves: 15.

Sequence under test:
1. Rxe6+ Kxe6

FEN after 1. Rxe6+:
8/4k3/4R3/8/8/8/8/K7 b - - 0 1

Dead-node FEN after 1... Kxe6:
8/8/4k3/8/8/8/8/K7 w - - 0 2

Integration oracle:
The root and intermediate node are live. The capture-evasion Kxe6 removes the
last rook and creates king versus king. The terminal check must run at every
quiescence node and before stand pat.

Expected result:
Dead final node; quiescence score 0; evaluation and stand pat not run; two
unmake operations restore both rooks and the turn.

Technical objective:
Verify detection within quiescence after a capture chain.

Status: VERIFIED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DPR-T14 — Checkmate regression against Black
Original position: 62 of 64
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEN:
7k/6Q1/5K2/8/8/8/8/8 b - - 0 1

Turn: Black. Check: yes. Legal moves: 0.

Precedence oracle:
The true terminal is checkmate in White's favor. The dead-position detector
must not turn the mating score into a draw or allow subsequent static
evaluation.

Expected result:
Checkmate true; stalemate false; terminal cause checkmate; mating score
against the side to move, adjusted for distance according to the engine's
convention; bestmove 0000.

Technical objective:
Confirm that the precedence and sign of the mating score remain intact.

Status: VERIFIED

8. CONCLUSION
==============================================================================

The sample brings together positive and negative cases, positions with and
without check, minimal and promoted material, pawn blockades, piece
confinement, en passant, a structural boundary, and terminal recognition at
the root, in alpha-beta, and in quiescence. It also protects checkmate
precedence.

The 16 tests published here represent the scope of alpha-010 without exposing
the complete internal inventory of 64 tests.
